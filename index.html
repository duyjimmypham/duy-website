Electron Configuration

Place electrons one at a time, from hydrogen through calcium. Compare the filling sequence with orbital boxes and configuration notation.

Add protons, neutrons, and electrons to see what changes the element, isotope, and charge. Covers hydrogen through neon.

Particle counts sit beside nuclear notation. The two-shell view is a counting model, not a picture of electron paths.
