Teaching

Chemistry simulations
  • Build an Atom. Add particles to make elements, isotopes, and ions.
  • Electron Configuration. Follow electrons into shells, subshells, and orbitals.
  • Covalent & Ionic Bonds. Follow electrons through bonding and ion formation.
  • Molecular Shape & Polarity. Turn molecules around and see how their dipoles add up.
  • Ideal Gas Law. Explore pressure, volume, and temperature in a gas.
  • Le Chatelier’s Principle. Stress an equilibrium and see which way it shifts.
  • Titration. Add base drop by drop and stop at the first lasting pink.
  • Acid and Base Strength. Dissolve a strong or weak acid or base and see how much ionizes.
